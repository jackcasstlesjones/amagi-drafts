07Pathway

One pathway, from first concern to annual review.

Each person has one status. It decides which form staff see, which checks run and what happens next, so nobody has to remember the protocol.

Pre-screen

Risk factors and everyday observations

Screening

A validated screen such as CIT-6

Referred

Structured GP referral summary

Waiting

Timed check-ins and escalation, so the wait is never silent

Diagnosed

Switches to post-diagnosis signals

Annual review

Tracked and prompted

09Product

Every flag reviewed by a person, on a deadline.

Care workers log what they see. The rules suggest an urgency, and a supervisor confirms or overrides it before the review window closes. Clients are pseudonymised throughout.
